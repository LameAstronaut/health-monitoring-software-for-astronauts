# Body view and alerts

The application is a synthetic demonstrator. It is not NASA-certified or validated for flight or clinical decisions.

Risk monitoring includes a synthetic astronaut illustration with keyboard-accessible body regions. Available measurements include cardiopulmonary readings, saved examination reports, recorded sleep/weight and reaction-test averages. Unsupported measurements remain explicitly unavailable.

The alert icon flashes with a one-second period. The full-screen background, text and evidence stay steady. **Stop flashing** and the operating system's reduced-motion preference disable the icon animation.
